# W2a-R licensed first draft · Verification record

The ten source files extracted from the Word manuscript contained 1,411 lines including the full MIT headers. Every recovered file equalled both the Markdown listing and its project source byte for byte. A build of all ten recovered modules completed successfully under Lean 4.36.0. They regenerated the same final Lean packet and the same raw compiler output as the licensed project. All 17 audited declarations passed, including three conditional physical or trajectory applications. The mathematical checkpoint before licensing was reconstructed independently and matched its previously reviewed byte stream.
